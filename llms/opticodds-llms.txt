# OpticOdds

> OpticOdds is a premier B2B sports betting data provider delivering real-time sportsbook odds, player props, alternate markets, injury data, and historical data from 200+ sportsbooks. Its platform powers sportsbook operators, traders, prediction markets, DFS operators, platform providers, sports betting apps, and media/affiliate businesses worldwide. Now part of Gambling.com Group.

OpticOdds' core offering is a low-latency Sports Betting API plus a suite of trading, automation, and intelligence products (Odds Screen, Copilot, Copilot Bet Builder, Sportsbook AI, Historical Odds). Developer documentation lives at developer.opticodds.com. The site is available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Korean, and Chinese — canonical URLs below are English.

## Core Products

- [Sports Betting API](https://opticodds.com/sports-betting-api): Real-time odds API aggregating 200+ sportsbooks, including player props, alternate markets, and injury data.
- [Odds Screen](https://opticodds.com/odds-screen): The fastest sportsbook live odds screen on the market, with custom consensus lines, real-time alerts, and live odds from 200+ sportsbooks.
- [Copilot](https://opticodds.com/copilot): Automated sportsbook trading and risk management software — generate custom odds, manage risk, and scale operations without increasing headcount.
- [Copilot Bet Builder](https://opticodds.com/copilot-bet-builder): High-performance bet builder and Same Game Parlay (SGP) engine for sportsbooks, with billions of correlated pricing combinations.
- [Sportsbook AI](https://opticodds.com/sportsbook-ai): Conversational odds data via Claude MCP — query live odds, player props, line movement, and cross-book comparisons with no code.
- [Historical Odds](https://opticodds.com/historical-odds): Years of timestamped, normalized historical betting odds across 200+ sportsbooks for backtesting, CLV analysis, and quant research.
- [API Integration](https://opticodds.com/api-integration): Overview of how operators plug OpticOdds into their platform quickly, with multiple formats and full developer docs.
- [OpenOdds Partnership Program](https://opticodds.com/openodds-program): Partnership program that markets and sells partner products to OpticOdds' customer base.
- [Developer Documentation](https://developer.opticodds.com/reference/getting-started): Full API reference and getting-started guides for developers.
